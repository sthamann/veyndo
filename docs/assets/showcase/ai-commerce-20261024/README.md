# Veyndo AI commerce film

Produced 24 October 2026. **1:56, English, silent, 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps.**

The film explains contextual merchant assistance, catalogue intake, design references, visual and coding-agent apps, flows, fictional customer scenarios, shopping by intent and the shared Rust commerce foundation. Actual product captures date from 15–22 October and retain their original pixels; focus crops, transitions and the architecture illustration are editorial. The interface captures are mostly German. Titles and burned-in captions are English; VTT and SRT are included.

The editable HyperFrames project includes the script, scene compositions, local assets, source hashes, storyboard and director specification. Existing screenshots are public synthetic data; shown payments are simulated. Film production is separate from dated software acceptance.

## Playback and checks

- MP4 / H.264 High Level 4.1, YUV420p, 1080p30, reference/bitrate constraints, no audio stream.
- Fast-start `moov` atom before `mdat`; 60-frame keyframe interval.
- Complete file decoded; 3,480 frames and 116 seconds verified.
- HyperFrames runtime, layout and contrast checks passed; beat, transition, phone and decoded-frame sheets inspected. Silence is intentional.
- Native video sources, `playsinline`, on-demand loading, English captions and a direct MP4 link are available on the homepage, team briefing and documentation.
